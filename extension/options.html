Should I Buy It?

Shows a verdict card on Walmart product pages, using these goals. Everything stays in this browser.

Bring your goals from the app

In the app, open Goals → Your data and choose Copy as text or Copy transfer link, then paste it here. Only your goals, skip list and mild-food setting are used.
