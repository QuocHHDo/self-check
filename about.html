How it works

Should I Buy It? · Free, no account, runs in your browser

Search for a food or scan a barcode, and the app shows its Nutrition Facts label and a verdict: does it fit your goals? The verdict uses the calories, protein, fiber and limits you set on the Goals tab. Until you set them, it uses standard ones: 2,000 calories, 100 g protein, 28 g fiber, and at most 20 g saturated fat, 2,300 mg sodium and 50 g added sugar a day.

It doesn't check allergens or medical needs. A “Get it” means the nutrition fits your goals, not that the food is safe for you. If you have a food allergy, read the ingredient list on the package. If you have a condition that affects what you eat (diabetes, kidney disease, pregnancy and others), follow your doctor's or dietitian's advice. This is general nutrition guidance, not medical advice.

How a verdict is decided

Each serving gets points for what helps and loses points for what works against your goals. Every point shows up as a reason under Why, and the What decided it chart shows how they add up.

Limit it−2 points or lower. Works against your goals as a regular buy.
In moderationIn between, or a good food with one real drawback.
Get it+2 points or higher, with nothing serious against it.
FineToo small to matter either way (a splash of sauce, a spice).
SkipIt matches something on your skip list.

What adds points

  • Protein for the calories: +1 when at least 20% of the calories come from protein, +2 at 40% (and at least 5 g).
  • Fiber: +2 at 2.8 g or more a serving (10% of the Daily Value, the FDA's “good source” bar).
  • Whole fruit, vegetables and whole grains: +2.
  • Unsaturated fats (nuts, avocado, olive oil, fatty fish) and lots of food for very few calories: small bonuses.

What takes points away

  • Saturated fat and added sugar: −1 when one serving is 15% of your daily limit, −2 at 30%.
  • Sodium: −1 at 20% of your daily limit, −2 at 35%.
  • Treats (desserts, candy, fried snacks): −1. Processed meat (bacon, deli meat, hot dogs): −2.
  • Empty calories: 100 calories or more with little protein or fiber. A big share of your day (20% of your calories) with little protein: −1.

A few safeguards

  • Missing numbers stay unknown, not zero. A product with no saturated-fat figure can't earn “Get it” just because the number is missing.
  • Meals on the Plate tab are judged against a third of your day. A meal that uses up a third of the day's saturated fat, sodium or added sugar can't be “Get it”.
  • The verdict is always for one standard serving. The servings buttons change the label and the bars, not the verdict.

Privacy

No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your goals and lists stay in your browser. See the privacy policy for exactly what's sent where.

Spot a wrong verdict or number?

Report it. Say which food, what the package label says, and what you expected. Photos of the label help most. (This opens GitHub, which needs a free account.)
