Privacy policy

Should I Buy It? (website and browser extension) · Last updated October 25, 2026

Short version: there are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing about you is collected, sold or shared. Your goals, lists and settings stay in your own browser.

What stays in your browser

Your goals, skip list, favorites, grocery list, plate, the prices you enter, foods you looked up, and the extension's settings are saved in your browser's own storage on your device. They are never sent to us; we run no server that could receive them. Clearing your browser data, or removing the extension, deletes them.

What the browser extension reads

The extension runs only on walmart.com. On a product page it reads that page's product information (name, barcode, category, ingredients and Nutrition Facts) to show its verdict. If you turn on search badges in its settings, it also opens the product pages of items in search results you scroll to, to read the same information. It does not read other sites, your browsing history, your Walmart account, your cart or anything you type.

What leaves your device, and only to look up nutrition

SentToWhen
A product's barcode numberOpen Food Facts, then USDA FoodData CentralOnly when a product page has no Nutrition Facts of its own. Sent without cookies.
What you search for, or a barcode you scanOpen Food Facts or USDA FoodData CentralOnly when you use the website's search or barcode lookup.

These are public nutrition databases. They receive the request like any website visit (including your IP address, as every web request does) and are covered by their own privacy policies. Nothing that identifies you is added to these requests.

Links you choose to open

Backup files, transfer links and "send to your list" links carry your saved data inside the file or the link itself, so they reach only whoever you give them to. The website reads them in your browser and asks before changing anything.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or transferring of data to anyone, and no use of data for anything other than showing nutrition verdicts.
  • No remote code: the extension runs only the code it was installed with.
