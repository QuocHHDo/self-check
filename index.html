    Walmart on your phone

    A bookmark that checks the Walmart product you're looking at. Tap it on a product page and this app opens with the verdict.

    1. Bookmark any page (this one is fine) and name it Should I Buy It?
    2. Edit that bookmark and replace its address with the bookmark code:
    3. On a Walmart product page: iPhone (Safari) open Bookmarks and tap it. Android (Chrome) type “Should I Buy It?” in the address bar and tap the bookmark in the suggestions.

    On a computer, drag this to your bookmarks bar instead: Should I Buy It? (or install the browser extension, which shows the verdict on the page itself).

    Built-in values are approximate typical figures for a standard serving; packaged foods vary by brand, so the label on the package wins. USDA results come from USDA FoodData Central; scanned products come from Open Food Facts, a crowd-sourced database that can be incomplete. The label's % Daily Value uses standard FDA values; the bars and verdict use your goals. General nutrition guidance, not medical advice. It doesn't check allergens.
